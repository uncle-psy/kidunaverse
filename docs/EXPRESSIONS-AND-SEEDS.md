# Expressions, Seeds, and Payloads

> Status: Layout Kit/product proposal. This document does not update the Kiduna Taxonomy Canon.

An **Expression** is a complete unit of material presented for exploration or experience. It may compose authorized Realms, Maps, Transmitters, Media, Nodes, Seeds, and related material while remaining distinct from its components, Records, provenance, and Sources.

A **Seed** is a bounded collection or starting unit. One or more Seeds may form a **Payload**. A Signal always keeps **Message** and **Payload** distinct. Opening or presenting a Seed may contribute to an Expression; Seed, Payload, transmission, Delivery Landing, Semantic Landing, and Expression are not synonyms. Existing package IDs may remain as compatibility identifiers, but the product label is Seed.

The Field supports search, format/place/source filters, recent/oldest sorting, saved items, visibility reasons, access boundaries, details, and opening in the assigned Layout. The member-facing Field intentionally uses ordinary language such as places, stories, tools, games, missions, movements, and purpose. Canonical implementation terms remain available in technical documentation and detailed inspection where they are genuinely useful.
