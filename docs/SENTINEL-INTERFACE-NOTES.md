# Sentinel Interface Notes

> Status: Layout Kit/product proposal. This document does not update the Kiduna Taxonomy Canon.

Sentinel configures Source-relative Coherence/Vibe ranges, content boundaries, verification, security, privacy, and reception boundaries. Presets include a legally and technically permitted fully Open adult setting; granular preferences cover age appropriateness, violence, trauma, sexual content, racial topics, harassment/abuse, graphic material, distressing subjects, and user-defined topics.

Settings affect reception, staging, warnings, and presentation; they do not silently rewrite or destroy material. Verification records evidence, provenance, disputes, corrections, uncertainty, and revocations without presenting verification as universal truth, moral worth, or unlimited trust.
